# Chargeback & Disputes Policy
## Monitoring thresholds (fictional internal thresholds, not real card-scheme values)
| Chargeback ratio (count, monthly) | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| below 0.65% | Normal | none |
| 0.65–0.9%   | Early warning | account manager review |
| above 0.9%  | Watchlist | no limit increases; remediation plan within 10 days |
| above 1.5%  | Excessive | rolling reserve raised to 15%; consider termination |
## Dispute handling
Evidence must be submitted within 20 calendar days of the dispute notification.
