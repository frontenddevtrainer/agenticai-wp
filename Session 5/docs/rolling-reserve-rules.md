# Rolling Reserve Rules
| Merchant risk tier | Reserve (% of daily settlement) | Hold period |
|---|---|---|
| Low    | 0%  | — |
| Medium | 5%  | 90 days |
| High   | 10% | 180 days |
High-risk MCCs (e.g. 7995 betting/gaming, 5967 direct marketing) are always High tier.
Releasing a reserve early requires Head of Merchant Risk approval.
